Timglas vs Toggl Track.

Toggl Track is one of the best-known time trackers: a cloud service with web, desktop and mobile apps, a free plan, and paid plans billed per user per month. It is built for individuals and teams who want their timers synced everywhere and connected to the rest of their tools.

Timglas is a menu-bar time tracker for one person on a Mac. No account, no cloud, one payment. Everything stays in a file on your disk.

Which one

  • Choose Toggl Track if

    You work in a team, need your timers on your phone and in the browser, or want time to flow into tools like project managers and invoicing apps.

  • Choose Timglas if

    You work alone on a Mac, want nothing to leave your machine, and would rather pay once than per month.
