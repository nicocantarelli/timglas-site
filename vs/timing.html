Timglas vs Timing.

Timing is a well-established automatic time tracker for Mac. It records which apps, documents and websites you use and helps you assign that activity to projects. It is sold as a subscription in several editions, with sync and a web app.

Timglas is a menu-bar time tracker for one person on a Mac. No account, no cloud, one payment. Everything stays in a file on your disk.

Which one

  • Choose Timing if

    You want a complete record of everything you do on your Mac, captured in the background, and you are happy to subscribe.

  • Choose Timglas if

    You want a timer you control, automation only where you set a rule, and an app you own outright after one payment.
